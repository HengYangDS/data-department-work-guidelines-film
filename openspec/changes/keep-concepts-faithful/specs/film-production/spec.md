## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Faithful Complete DDWG Film

The production SHALL explain the current canonical charter and all six topics
as an intelligible department-wide work-quality contract. It SHALL preserve
source distinctions, naturally explain terms before relying on them, and use
bounded illustrative examples without implying deployment or adoption.

#### Scenario: Viewer understands the relationship among topics

- **WHEN** a viewer follows the opening, six topics and closing
- **THEN** evidence, judgment, authority, delivery and improvement form a coherent argument
- **AND** no mandatory sequential pipeline or narrow business scope is implied

#### Scenario: Viewer follows the complete narrative

- **WHEN** a viewer watches either delivered language edition
- **THEN** judgment, data, delivery, human–AI collaboration, communication
  and practice evolution are intelligible without undefined specialist terms
- **AND** the ending returns naturally to the opening and DDWG's purpose

#### Scenario: Each sentence is faithful, clear on first hearing and idiomatic

- **WHEN** an independent language review reads every thought and label of both
  editions against its source passage and the film's concept contract
- **THEN** fidelity, concept fidelity, first-hearing clarity and idiom each score
  at least 90, with no material finding
- **AND** compressed formulas and calques are replaced or explained in plain words,
  so a rule is heard as what people do rather than as an abstract formula
- **AND** each source term keeps its exact counterpart in the words a quantitative
  hedge fund's data department uses, explained once where needed and never replaced
  by a nearby everyday word
- **AND** each idea keeps one word across the edition

#### Scenario: A first-time viewer keeps each point

- **WHEN** independent listeners from different parts of the department, outside
  the topic's specialty, hear each thought of an edition once and in order
- **THEN** no thought loses its point, and every part that changes who decides,
  what is allowed or what counts as done is still heard
- **AND** a lost point, one idea heard under different words, or a source term
  rendered by a word that changes its meaning or register is a material finding of
  the final review

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Concepts keep their source definitions

The film SHALL keep a concept contract that binds each guideline concept the
narration, captions or picture name, sense by sense, to its passage at the pinned
commit, its essence and one approved rendering in each edition, and both editions
SHALL be composed from that contract rather than translated from each other. Each
segment SHALL declare the concept senses it states, both editions of a segment
SHALL declare the same senses, and an edition SHALL name a sense only by its
approved rendering.

#### Scenario: A rendering that departs from the contract refuses

- **WHEN** the scripts and the picture are checked before narration
- **THEN** each sense a segment declares appears in that segment by its approved
  rendering
- **AND** a counterexample the contract records for a sense, or a sense declared
  in one edition of a segment only, refuses and is named

#### Scenario: A quotation that no longer matches its source refuses

- **WHEN** the contract is verified against a clone of the guidelines repository
- **THEN** each quoted passage is found verbatim in its document at the pinned commit
- **AND** a passage that is not found refuses and is named

### Requirement: Declared readings hold in every occurrence

Every Chinese character whose dictionary reading is ambiguous SHALL have its
reading in context declared in the pronunciation contract, and every name SHALL
have one declared spoken form. Every occurrence of a declared reading or name
SHALL match the approved reference occurrence of that reading or name.

#### Scenario: An undeclared ambiguous reading refuses

- **WHEN** the Chinese script is checked before narration
- **THEN** every character with more than one dictionary reading has a declared
  reading for its context
- **AND** an occurrence without one refuses and is named with its segment and
  position

#### Scenario: Every occurrence matches its reference

- **WHEN** an edition's narration is qualified
- **THEN** each occurrence of a declared reading or name matches its approved
  reference occurrence within the threshold its check was calibrated to
- **AND** a mismatch is retaken before the user listens to the narration
