## Context

Edition 1.3.0 passed review round 40 without a finding. This Change repairs that round's notes and proves the release's reproduction from a clean checkout. The film's method, narrator, runtime and argument do not change, so the design of the Change that adopted the excellence criteria still describes the film; this document records only the decisions that the repairs need.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Repair every note of round 40 that the film owns, each against its own reverification line.
- Take from the engine the measures and gates that eight notes were deferred to, by adopting its release.
- Calibrate the picture floors with a rebuild that owes nothing to the production build.

**Non-Goals:**

- No change of what the guidelines say: a question about the source goes to its owner, and the film follows the answer.
- No film-local substitute for a measure or gate the engine owns.

## Decisions

1. **The published review records own the notes.** Tasks cite a note by its id; the observation, repair and reverification lines are read in the review records of release 1.3.0. A copy in this Change would be a second record that could drift.
2. **Text is settled before any narration, and the English additions are taken in the order the runtime allows.** The complete bilingual text of every changed thought is adopted, read against its source lines and rechecked independently for first hearing before a take is made. The two editions have different room. In release 1.3.0 the English gaps already stand at their lower bounds (22 paragraph gaps of 1.01 s, 7 chapter gaps of 1.84 s) and its 130 thought pauses average 0.83 s, so every added English second has to come from text that gets shorter or from thought pauses; the Chinese gaps stand about 17 s above their bounds (paragraph gaps of 1.43 s against 1.1 s, chapter gaps of 3.32 s against 2.0 s). The repairs the notes ask for add about 37 English words, some 14 s. They are therefore taken in this order, each only while the fitted soundtrack keeps its thought pauses within the declared range and its boundary rule: the idiom of 07.6.2 reworded at equal length (EN40-10); the three qualifiers (EN40-1); the split of 11.10.1, which removes a picture cut inside a sentence (EN40-8); the verbs of 14.2 (EN40-4); the charter's two clauses (Z40-4). A clause that does not fit stays a declared note in both languages, so that the editions say the same things.
3. **A source question is the source owner's.** 14.3 keeps the faithful term and 21.2 the contract's words until the owner of the guidelines answers; the film does not resolve the source's ambiguity on its own.
4. **A retake needs evidence that it is nearer.** 13.2 is retaken only against pair references that separate; 18.3 and 21.2 only if listening hears the wrong syllable. Auditions for pace and phrase ends are judged by the notes' own measures within the edition's runtime.
5. **Labels use the voice's words or are declared.** Two of the eight labels of Z40-5 are set in the words spoken at their moment (14.4 上报、交给人接管或停下, 24.3 汇报、答复、写纪要和上报). Six are declared: 07.7 只找有利的证据 and 11.6 偏差可能藏在别处 keep their condensed names, because the voice's clause is the whole of its caption cue (只找对自己有利的证据； and 偏差还可能藏在别的地方：), a label that repeats a cue whole is refused by the caption-echo gate, and any run of the clause short of it loses its verb or its subject (对自己有利的证据, 还可能藏在别的地方); 22.1 成员和智能体 and 22.4 智能体 name the film's recurring figures where neither voice names an actor; 22.2 再做下去有害 is the kind's name within its rail's wrap, with the four harms drawn as its marks on their words; 29.8 什么情况回滚 is shortened because the voice's words repeat the caption cue 什么情况下回滚。 whole, which the caption-echo gate refuses.
6. **Glides are timed by distance.** The glide peak falls to 420 px/s and each glide takes the time its distance needs; the film declares the notes' line of 450 px/s as its pace limit, so the picture gate refuses a faster stretch. On the staged pictures the motion audit listed stretches over the line of three kinds, and each is recut at its cause. Two moves that compound become one: a row's step and lean on one word are one glide timed by the way the row makes (13), rides to stops named sooner than a glide reaches them are one ride through them at the glide's pace (14, 23), and a part that docks or a flow that runs on a gliding stage, the way the stage glides, gives its own pace up to the glide's (14, 19, 23). A mark is not carried by a drawing tip or by a way whose ends move: the latest price stands where the history ends and inks in as the head arrives (11), and no point travels a way while its ends part (15). A move set by a fixed ease is timed by its distance: 17's row step and its claim's move back while a signature draws on, 19's Agent, and 21's page reflow and seal, which is at rest before its thought ends. A move bounded by its words takes the shortest way: 10.1's price lifts from the table's cell nearest the rail's first station, the first of its last row, and flies on the film's clock within its thought, since the next thought's first words name the station it lands on. From the table's middle cell the English way was 787 px for the 1.81 s the thought leaves, about 495 px/s however it was eased; from the nearest cell it is about 700 px, flown at about 430 px/s, and the Chinese flight, about 610 px in a thought of 2.19 s, keeps the glide's pace. No stretch is declared as an exception.
7. **A row keeps a minimum gap between names.** Neighbouring names on one row stand at least four word spaces apart; long names wrap over two lines or the row's pitch widens. The engine's geometry check probes the gap, so the rule does not rest on a review script.
8. **Engine measures come from the engine.** Thought levelling, typeset timeline text, clarity frame assignment, the stopped-end gate and the transient test of the two masters are adopted with the `filmkit` release that offers them, and the film declares its values in `content/film.toml`.
9. **The floors come from a clean checkout.** The rebuild that calibrates them is made from an anonymous clone of the `v1.3.0` tag with no earlier output, following the README.
10. **13.2's retake rests on recognition; the pair of sounds cannot decide it (EN40-3).** Recognition wrote "convenience schema" for the delivered take and writes "convenient schema" for seed 11107128, as it does for all sixteen takes of the audition. The pair check narrates the thought as written and with the competing word, two seeds each, and asks first whether those references separate. They do not: by the word's spectrum and pitch 0 of 4 references stand nearer their own reading, and by the length of the hiss before "schema", which a second /s/ would lengthen, 1 of 4 (80 and 90 ms as written, 94 and 88 ms with the competing word). The voice does not release the /t/, so the two readings differ by less than two seeds of one reading do. The measure can therefore support neither take, and decision 4's evidence for this retake is the recognition alone.
11. **17.1.2 and 21.4.2 keep their takes; their pace is the sentences' own (EN40-5).** In seventeen takes each, "The deliverable is at the agreed location." lasts 1.76 to 1.92 s and "A majority opinion is not evidence." 1.68 to 1.84 s, against 1.84 s and 1.76 s in the delivered takes. No take reaches the note's line of 6.6 syllables per second, since the voice reads a short declarative between longer sentences at this pace; a slower reading would need other words, and both sentences say their source exactly.
12. **The timeline's caption text is typeset; its narration fields keep the script's bytes (EN40-9).** `filmkit` 0.22 writes each caption cue as it is drawn, so the timeline's caption text holds no straight apostrophe, where release 1.3.0 held 27. The segment text, spoken text, thoughts and words of the timeline are the script's own characters, which each recording's text digest binds; they are read by the engine and never shown, and typesetting them would release every take whose thought holds an apostrophe.
13. **The charter's two clauses stay declared in both editions (Z40-4).** The clause that a project may clarify the order of authority without gaining authority to waive, and the clause that end-to-end responsibility must not dissolve into "everyone", would add about 26 English words, some 9 s of speech. The engine's rules would admit them: the English soundtrack assembles with a statement pause as low as 0.60 s. But this edition already takes 0.02 s from each of the 130 English statement pauses for the fifteen words of its other repairs, the two clauses would take 0.07 s more (0.79 s to 0.72 s), and the owner lengthened the films to 25 minutes because the narration was too fast. The English pauses already stand under the Chinese ones (a median of 0.79 s against 0.89 s). The clauses therefore wait for a text batch that frees English words; the second is said in substance in both editions (04.3 and 13.4 in English, 04.3 and 13.5 in Chinese), and no statement of the film is made wrong by the omission.
14. **In the English edition each segment stands within 0.2 dB of its chapter's level.** With each thought within 0.4 dB of its segment, the largest step between adjacent thoughts inside a segment is 0.81 LU, but two segments of one chapter could still stand 1.5 dB apart, and the step into 14.1 stood at 1.37 LU, over the notes' line of 1.2 LU across a pause. `filmkit` 0.22.3 levels each segment within a declared tolerance of its chapter; at 0.2 dB the largest step across segments is 0.90 LU (into 26.1). The Chinese edition declares none: its largest step across segments is 0.87 LU.
15. **Three English cues take authored breaks, and one stays declared by name.** The engine listed four English cues above 25 characters per second, each as release 1.3.0 delivered it. A script may author a segment's cue breaks, so three are broken where their sentences allow a slower reading: "Within those limits" and "comes what the decision owner for this matter explicitly decides." (23.7 and 20.9, was 25.5; with "comes" on the first cue the second would repeat a label of the picture whole, which the picture gate refuses); "but must stay out of sharing, production and decisions" and "until qualified for that use." (24.1 and 18.2, was 25.9); "If the agreed conditions are unmet, the work goes back" and "to the member for revision within the delegation boundary." (22.2 and 22.8, was 28.6). "or a production write would be needed." stays at 25.2: the next sentence follows at once, and each break that would read slower splits a clause, a subject from its verb or a noun from its article, which reads worse than a cue 0.2 characters per second over the line.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The English edition has little free time. → Runtime is fitted from gaps and commas as before, never by deleting necessary punctuation or forcing pace; a clause that cannot fit stays a declared note.
- The engine release may change bytes of the soundtrack beyond the notes' thoughts. → The release checks and continuity measures run on the whole soundtrack, and both editions are reviewed again.
- Lower glide peaks lengthen moves and can push a change into a thought pause. → Each retimed glide keeps the quiet-join rule, and the motion audit runs on both editions before any render.
- The clean rebuild could show a film below its recorded floor. → The manifest then records the measured floor after the lowest frames are inspected side by side, as the reproduction requirement says.
